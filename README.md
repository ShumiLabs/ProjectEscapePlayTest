# Project Escape — playtest build

A top-down dig-and-blast action puzzle. You are underground, everything around
you can be dug through or blown up, and the people looking for you are quite
good at it.

**Working title.** The real name is not settled, so this one is a placeholder.

---

## Download

### ➡️ [Get the latest build](../../releases/latest)

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. About 37 MB zipped. No installer — unzip it anywhere and
run `ProjectEscape.exe`.

There is no Mac or Linux build. Sorry.

### Windows will stop you the first time

You will get a blue box saying **"Windows protected your PC"**.

> Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

That happens because the file is not code-signed yet — signing certificates cost
money and this is a prototype being handed to about five people. It is not a
sign that anything is wrong with the download. Your antivirus may grumble for
the same reason; if it quarantines the file, please tell me rather than fighting
it, because that is genuinely useful to know.

## What I need from you

**20–40 minutes.** Start a **New Campaign** — it opens on a short training
mission, then the first real one. That is the part I care about most.

After that, keep going if you want. There are thirty levels, an endless mode and
a two-player mode in there. They are much rougher and almost none of them have
been played by anybody. Wander if you feel like it — just tell me which bit you
mean.

Play it the way you would play something you had bought. **If you get stuck,
stay stuck for a while before giving up.** How you get unstuck is the single
thing I am most trying to learn.

### Then tell me

Not a checklist. Just play, then say whatever you remember.

1. **Talk me through anything you tried that nobody told you to try.**
   Especially if it worked. Especially if it did not.
2. Was there a moment where you thought *"oh, I could do X"*? What was X?
3. **Would you have played another level?** Honest answer. "No" is a genuinely
   useful result and will not hurt my feelings.
4. Anything you expected to be able to do and could not.
5. Anything that felt unfair, unclear, or like the game breaking.

What you *did* is more useful to me than what you thought of it. If you can
record yourself, or just scribble notes as you go, even better.

### If something breaks

Tell me what you were doing and roughly when. Press **Esc** and screenshot the
pause panel — the build number is in the bottom right, which tells me exactly
which version you had.

Saves and logs live in:

```
%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Fusebreak\
```

(Named after the project rather than the build — see "working title" above.)

If the game ever loses your progress there will be a file in there ending
`.unreadable`. Please send me that one; it is the only record of what happened.

## Controls

On screen at the bottom left the whole time, and **every one of them can be
rebound** in Options → Controls — keyboard and controller separately. Press
**H** to hide the reminder once you are sick of looking at it.

---

## Things I already know

Please do not spend your feedback on these:

- **All the art and audio is generated by code** rather than drawn or recorded,
  so it looks and sounds like exactly that. Do say if something is hard to
  **read**, though — that is a real problem, not a matter of taste.
- No music in the menus.
- Levels past the first few have never had a design pass.
- It has never run on any machine except mine.
- The name.

Everything else is fair game. Please be blunt — a polite playtest is a wasted
one.
